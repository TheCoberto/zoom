## Description

Java players have had zoom mods for years. Now Bedrock has one too, on every device. Double-tap Sneak and your view smoothly zooms in, so you can scout a far-off base, spot mobs across a valley or take better screenshots. No experiments needed, and normal sneaking still works.

Double-tap **Sneak** to zoom in. Double-tap again to zoom back out.

- **Keyboard**: Shift (or whatever Sneak is bound to)
- **Controller**: B / Circle
- **Touch**: the Sneak button

Why Sneak? Bedrock add-ons can't add new keybinds, and double-tapping Sneak works the same on every platform without getting in the way of normal sneaking.

### Installation

1. Download the .mcaddon and open it.
2. Create or edit a world and turn on the Zoom behavior pack. No experiments needed.
